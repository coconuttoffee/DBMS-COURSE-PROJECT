Entity relationship summary

Core tables

Table Purpose Key fields
Sites Stores each construction site and location details. site_id, name, location
Materials Lists materials used across projects with reorder rules. material_id, name, unit, reorder_level
Vendors Tracks suppliers used for purchase orders. vendor_id, name, contact_no
Purchase_Orders Stores purchase order header data and approval status. po_id, vendor_id, site_id, order_date, status
PO_Details Captures all materials on each purchase order. po_detail_id, po_id, material_id, quantity
Material_Transactions Acts as the stock ledger for all IN and OUT movements. transaction_id, site_id, material_id, type, quantity, date
Labor Maintains worker and pay information such as hourly rate and role. worker_id, name, role, hourly_rate
Attendance Records daily presence and calculates partial-day work. attendance_id, worker_id, date, status
Users Stores platform users and access roles. user_id, username, role
