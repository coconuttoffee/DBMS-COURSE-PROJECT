What the product does

Built for the daily rhythm of a job site.

Stock control

Track material balances on each site and review the in and out ledger before orders are placed.

Purchase orders

Raise orders to vendors, approve the request, and mark delivery when stock arrives on site.

Attendance

Record Present, Half-day or Absent for each worker and keep daily site coverage visible.

Wages

Calculate wages due for a date range with simple daily rates and partial day adjustments.

Low-stock alerts

Flag materials near or below reorder levels so the team reacts before a shortage affects the project.

Built for small teams

Roles keep site work visible without extra admin overhead.

  • Admin controls users and setup.
  • Site Manager reviews stock and attendance.
  • Store Keeper records stock movement.
  • Viewer reads reporting data.
